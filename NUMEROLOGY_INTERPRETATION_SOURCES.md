# Life Path interpretive notes — 2026-10-29

Scope: Modern Pythagorean-style numerology. Life Path arithmetic and symbolic
interpretations are separate evidence records. These are **not** scientifically
validated personality tests or predictions of an individual's future.

## Calculation and school
- Hans Decoz, *Life Path Number*, https://www.worldnumerology.com/numerology-life-path/
  describes reducing month/day/year separately and retaining 11, 22 and 33.
- Numerology.com, *Life Path Number*, https://www.numerology.com/articles/your-numerology-chart/life-path-number-meanings/
  provides a separate exposition. Other schools may reduce differently.
- Our engine retains this explicit school choice, not a universal claim.

## Boundaries
The title "Pythagorean-style" describes a modern numerology convention,
not a documented claim that Pythagoras authored these exact meanings.
Numbers do not establish an individual's talents, health, worth, partner,
career, lifespan, finances, or specific future events. Other numerology
schools can disagree; no cross-tradition predictive synthesis is made.
This short corpus is an early interpretive layer, not the final complete
source-critical astrology reading encyclopedia.
